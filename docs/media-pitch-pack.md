# HiredNext Media Pitch Pack

These are publication-specific starting angles, not copy-paste press releases. Each pitch should be adapted to the journalist’s current beat and should use only evidence HiredNext can substantiate.

## 1. AI screening is becoming the first recruiter — where human judgement must interrupt it

**Targets:** ETHRWorld, ET Edge Insights, People Matters

**Pitch:** AI can shortlist at a scale no recruiter can match, but the most consequential hiring errors often happen when a clean data signal is mistaken for judgement. HiredNext can explain which parts of screening are genuinely improved by parsing/matching automation and which signals — motivation, context, leadership judgement, career trade-offs — still require human conversation.

**HiredNext contribution:** Practical recruiter examples of where automation saves time, where false positives/negatives appear, and a framework for deciding when a human should take over.

**Evidence rule:** Use anonymised recurring patterns unless there is a clean dataset behind a number.

## 2. Skills-first hiring fails when companies still write activity-first job descriptions

**Targets:** Financial Express, India Today, ETHRWorld

**Pitch:** Employers increasingly say they want skills-first hiring, but many job descriptions still list years, pedigree and generic responsibilities rather than measurable outcomes. That disconnect makes both human screening and AI matching noisier. HiredNext can show how role calibration changes shortlist quality before sourcing even begins.

**HiredNext contribution:** A practical “outcomes vs activity” job-description framework and examples of adjacent experience that employers often overlook.

## 3. GCC hiring is moving from scale to specialised capability teams

**Targets:** People Matters, ETHRWorld, GCC-focused business media

**Pitch:** The next GCC hiring challenge is not simply adding headcount; it is finding specialised leaders and teams that can own products, analytics, finance, engineering or transformation work. Exact-match hiring narrows the market unnecessarily, so adjacent-sector and adjacent-capability mapping becomes more important.

**HiredNext contribution:** Search calibration, role adjacency, cross-border stakeholder assessment and India talent-market observations.

## 4. India retail leadership: expansion is easier than building leadership benches

**Targets:** Indian Retailer, Retail4Growth, business media

**Pitch:** Retail expansion can outpace the supply of credible store, cluster, regional and functional leaders. The hiring problem is not only vacancy filling; it is whether organisations have enough managers who can translate central strategy into store execution and omnichannel performance.

**HiredNext contribution:** Patterns in leadership progression, P&L evidence, store-to-region succession and what makes candidates portable across retail formats.

## 5. Garment and textile growth needs stronger plant and export leadership pipelines

**Targets:** Apparel Resources, manufacturing/business media

**Pitch:** Garment and textile companies compete on cost, quality, delivery and increasingly technology, but senior hiring still depends on a relatively narrow pool of leaders who can combine technical depth with people, compliance and commercial execution. HiredNext can discuss how adjacent-industry mapping and structured evidence can widen the viable leadership pool.

**HiredNext contribution:** Anonymised search observations from garment/textile recruitment, leadership capability themes and mobility constraints.

## 6. NBFC automation is shrinking repetitive work while raising demand for judgement-heavy roles

**Targets:** ETBFSI, financial/business media

**Pitch:** Automation does not simply reduce roles; it changes where human value sits. In NBFCs and financial services, repetitive processing can decline while demand rises for people who can make risk, collections, customer, analytics, compliance and operating decisions with context.

**HiredNext contribution:** Recruiter view on changing role specifications, skills adjacency and how to assess judgement rather than keyword familiarity.

## 7. The megamanager follow-up: flatter structures can quietly create overloaded managers

**Targets:** News18, CNBC-TV18, workplace/business desks

**Pitch:** Removing management layers can speed decisions, but the work of those layers does not disappear. It gets redistributed. HiredNext can explain why organisations should distinguish bureaucracy reduction from management capacity, and what candidate behaviour reveals when manager roles become too broad.

**HiredNext contribution:** Hiring-market observations on span of control, candidate expectations and signs that a “bigger” manager role has become structurally unmanageable.

## 8. AI-written CV versus AI-screened CV: authenticity is becoming a new hiring signal

**Targets:** India Today, Financial Express, ETHRWorld

**Pitch:** Candidates can now generate polished CVs in minutes while employers increasingly use automated screening. When both sides optimise for machines, language becomes less useful as evidence. Recruiters have to look for verifiable scope, outcomes, decisions and context instead.

**HiredNext contribution:** A recruiter checklist for separating polished wording from demonstrated capability, plus advice for candidates on using AI without erasing their own evidence.

## 9. Why offer acceptance is no longer the end of the hiring process

**Targets:** People Matters, ETHRWorld, Financial Express

**Pitch:** Notice periods, counteroffers, location, family decisions and competing processes make the post-offer period one of the highest-risk parts of recruitment. HiredNext can explain why closure governance should be designed at mandate stage rather than treated as last-mile coordination.

**HiredNext contribution:** Anonymised recruiter observations on offer-to-join risk factors and practical employer/candidate communication routines.

## 10. Human-led AI-enabled hiring: the governance question companies are missing

**Targets:** ET Edge Insights, ETHRWorld, People Matters

**Pitch:** The useful question is not “AI or recruiter?” but “which decisions can be automated, which can be assisted, and who remains accountable?” HiredNext can provide a practical governance model covering AI screening, automated outreach/calling, interview intelligence, psychometrics when appropriate and final human recommendation.

**HiredNext contribution:** A decision-rights framework that aligns directly with HiredNext’s human-led, technology-enabled recruitment position.
