# HiredNext candidate email standard

Taru requested this standard on 14 September 2026 for the email body and all future candidate correspondence.

- Body font: `Aptos, 'Segoe UI', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif`, 16px, line-height 1.7. Use the available fallback on the recipient's device; do not load remote web fonts.
- HiredNext navy: `#0c3466`. Orange accent: `#ff4e16`. Main reading text: `#172033`. White content background; muted outer background `#f4f6f9`.
- Keep headings navy and use orange sparingly for the brand accent. Use short paragraphs, 18px paragraph spacing and a 640px maximum content width with comfortable mobile padding.
- Send HTML with inline styles and a plain-text alternative. Use the shared `App\Services\HiredNextEmail` renderer for website mail. Existing subjects, recipients, threads, prices, attachments and delivery controls stay with their service owner.
- For personal Gmail replies, apply the same font, size, spacing and navy/orange signature in the HTML body. Keep correspondence concise; a short reply needs only a compact branded signature, not a large campaign header.
- Use **HiredNext Recruitment** as the brand. No unrelated colour themes, script fonts, image-only body copy, tracking pixels or claims of personal founder review.
- Never resend a previous candidate message merely to update its appearance.

An internal style preview is sent once to jobs with reference `HN-EMAIL-STYLE-1`. It is not an enquiry, order or sale.

## Central LinkedIn and Gmail conversation policy

HIREDNEXT REVENUE CONVERSATION STANDARD — TARU, 15 SEPTEMBER 2026 — HN-CONVERSATION-v1
Apply this standard to every authorised LinkedIn or Gmail revenue message, reply, draft and follow-up. The central owner is HiredNext Operations Control (6aa1712cd8508191a2ed76eae86835e9); existing specialist owners remain responsible for their own conversations.

1. Read the actual latest message, full relevant thread and available sent/queued history first. Answer what the person asked immediately. A package or price question gets the verified price, inclusions and relevant next step without a forced discovery conversation.
2. For outreach, start with a real, verified reason this person is relevant: their stated need, actual comment, role, vacancy or company development. Do not invent familiarity, a personal review, hiring difficulty, client interest or a relationship.
3. Give useful value before the offer. For CVs, cite one or two material observations from the CV actually reviewed and explain the hiring-manager implication. Distinguish missing evidence from missing experience; acknowledge a strong CV. For employers, use the verified mandate or hiring signal and a relevant hiring insight, without treating a news announcement as a mandate.
4. Ask at most one useful discovery question per conversational reply, and only when needed. Reuse information already supplied. This does not restrict an agreed paid-service intake questionnaire or necessary operational facts. A customer ready to proceed should receive the next step directly.
5. Offer one appropriate next action once the need is clear: an optional assessment, rebuild, practical correction or brief founder conversation. Do not force a call or send several competing CTAs. Verify current approved prices, service availability and scope. Paid CV work never purchases recruitment consideration, an interview or placement. Preserve service-specific restrictions; paid-service and pilot-interest threads do not become unrelated sales campaigns.
6. Use simple English, short paragraphs and a warm, direct, senior HiredNext voice. Keep approved sender identities, original logo and existing navy/orange email styling. Avoid copied banter, guilt, pressure, false urgency, generic flattery and unearned claims. Never use financial hardship as a sales lever.
7. Continue the conversation rather than restarting the pitch. Preserve one owner and canonical contact/thread IDs across channels. Check deduplication, current order status and consent before contact. Replies leave the automated no-response sequence for the appropriate owner; opt-outs suppress promotion. Preserve existing follow-up limits; add no extra touches or channel switches through this policy.
8. Measure meaningful replies, qualified conversations, agreed meetings, proposals and verified payments separately in existing records. Record last message, stated need, next action and owner when the current interface supports it. Never claim CRM synchronisation without an actual receipt.

This policy changes communication quality only. It does not create send permission, broaden recipients, convert read-only tasks into senders, alter exact approved content, bypass a delivery reservation, change schedules/triggers, restart paused work, add spend or create another controller/database. The employer Revenue Engine trial remains paused until Taru explicitly restarts it. Apply these rules within each task's existing scope; preserve narrower service and delivery requirements.
