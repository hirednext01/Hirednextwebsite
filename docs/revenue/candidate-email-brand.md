## Central LinkedIn and Gmail conversation policy

1. Read the actual latest message, full relevant thread and available sent/queued history first. Answer what the person asked immediately. A package or price question gets the verified price, inclusions and relevant next step without a forced discovery conversation.
2. For outreach, start with a real, verified reason this person is relevant: their stated need, actual comment, role, vacancy or company development. Do not invent familiarity, a personal review, hiring difficulty, client interest or a relationship.

Avoid copied banter, guilt, pressure, false urgency, generic flattery and unearned claims. Never use financial hardship as a sales lever.
7. Continue the conversation rather than restarting the pitch. Preserve one owner and canonical contact/thread IDs across channels. Check deduplication, current order status and consent before contact. Replies leave the automated no-response sequence for the appropriate owner; opt-outs suppress promotion. Preserve existing follow-up limits; add no extra touches or channel switches through this policy.
8. Measure meaningful replies, qualified conversations, agreed meetings, proposals and verified payments separately in existing records. Record last message, stated need, next action and owner when the current interface supports it. Never claim CRM synchronisation without an actual receipt.
