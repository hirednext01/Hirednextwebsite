# Commercial guardrails for new HiredNext offers

Effective 17 September 2026.

## Margin rule

Every new offer must target at least a 50% contribution margin after indirect tax and attributable delivery cost.

`net revenue = collected price / (1 + applicable GST rate)`

`contribution margin = (net revenue - attributable cost) / net revenue`

At ₹6,999 inclusive of 18% GST, net revenue is approximately ₹5,931.36. The attributable cost ceiling for a 50% contribution margin is ₹2,965.68 per completed order. Attributable cost includes paid media allocated to the sale, mentor or specialist fees, AI/API usage, workflow hosting, payment cost, delivery time and support.

Do not publish a new mentoring class or automation package until its expected cohort or order economics meet this rule at the minimum viable attendance or sales volume.

## Client automation ownership

Automation projects are built for the client and handed over in the client’s own n8n instance, cloud account and connected business accounts. HiredNext may configure and test the workflows from its delivery environment, then export or install the approved workflow for the client. The client owns its software subscriptions, API usage, credentials and ongoing operating charges unless a separate managed-support agreement says otherwise.

Never place a client’s production credentials inside a shared HiredNext workflow. State the required n8n and model/API subscriptions before purchase. A ChatGPT subscription is not represented as an OpenAI API allowance.
