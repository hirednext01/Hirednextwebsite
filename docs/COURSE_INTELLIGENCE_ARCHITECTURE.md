# HiredNext Career Gap to Learning Architecture

Assessment first. Recommendation second. Commerce third.

A course must never be shown only because a keyword exists in a CV.

## Flow
1. Capture current role, industry, location, compensation, experience, target role, target industry, target compensation and aspiration.
2. Parse the CV for evidence and capability signals.
3. Classify gaps as positioning, evidence, capability, industry knowledge, technology, commercial, leadership, certification, visibility or interview communication.
4. Positioning and visibility gaps do not trigger courses.
5. Capability gaps can be matched only to active, quality approved courses.
6. Show no more than three recommendations and explain why each was selected.
7. Track clicks, enrolments and completions.
8. Reassess after completion so new evidence can flow back into the CV, LinkedIn and interview narrative.

## Provider modes
Providers can connect through API catalogue, scheduled JSON or CSV feed, signed webhook events, affiliate or referral links, or a manually curated catalogue.

Webhooks are for events such as enrolment and completion. They are not required for catalogue ingestion.

## Quality rules
HiredNext should review curriculum, faculty and outcomes before a partner is marked approved. A complimentary audit seat should be requested wherever possible.

## Matching guardrails
No course for a pure wording problem.
No job or salary guarantee.
No recommendation only because a candidate wants a higher salary.
Prefer the smallest credible intervention.
Human approval can be required for senior profiles.
