# HiredNext 90-Day Media + SEO/AEO/GEO Authority Plan

## Baseline already earned

HiredNext has a credible earned-media base to build on: 10 verified coverage pieces across 8 unique media brands in the supplied November 2025–January 2026 coverage reports. The strongest recurring authority themes are labour-market change, reskilling, AI + human recruitment, skills-first hiring, staffing models, campus hiring, flatter organisations/megamanagers, and career/workforce decisions.

This plan does not replace or redesign the website. It turns existing evidence and future practitioner insight into a coherent authority graph around HiredNext and founder Taru Shikha.

## Strategic position

**HiredNext = human-led, technology-enabled recruitment expertise.**

The entity relationship we want search and answer engines to repeatedly encounter is:

**Taru Shikha → HiredNext Recruitment → executive search / leadership hiring → AI-assisted human judgement → India → sector hiring intelligence → independently published media commentary.**

We will prioritise original practitioner observations, verified external evidence, clear authorship, and publication-specific pitches. We will not manufacture client names, percentages, hiring outcomes, salary data or market statistics.

## Days 1–30 — Consolidate authority and win reactive commentary

### Owned authority
- Keep Home, Blog and Services design locked.
- Surface verified media coverage through the existing Press & Media page without replacing existing database entries.
- Create a founder authority page for Taru Shikha with ProfilePage/Person structured data and verified external coverage.
- Maintain a machine-readable media authority manifest and strengthen sitemap/LLM discovery.
- Use author-led blog content where the observation genuinely comes from Taru Shikha; avoid anonymous thought leadership when a named practitioner can own it.

### Media activity
Prioritise fast expert-response opportunities around:
- AI screening and where recruiter judgement must interrupt automation.
- Skills-first hiring versus degree- and pedigree-first hiring.
- AI-written CVs meeting AI screening systems.
- Manager-layer compression and the “megamanager” problem.
- Offer drop-offs, notice periods, counteroffers and candidate decision behaviour.

### Priority outlets
ETHRWorld, ET Edge Insights, People Matters, Financial Express, India Today, CNBC-TV18, News18 and Outlook Money.

## Days 31–60 — Build sector authority

Move beyond general HR commentary and make HiredNext useful in sectors where it can speak from recruitment practice.

### NBFC and financial services

Primary targets: ETBFSI and financial/business media.

### GCC, engineering and manufacturing
Pitch:

- Why adjacent-sector mapping matters for hard-to-fill engineering and manufacturing roles.

Primary targets: People Matters, ETHRWorld and GCC/manufacturing business publications.

## Days 61–90 — Create proprietary HiredNext Hiring Intelligence

Publish a small number of evidence-led briefs based only on data HiredNext can substantiate. Good formats:
- **HiredNext Hiring Signal:** 3–5 recurring observations from active mandates in one sector.
- **Recruiter Field Note:** a specific hiring problem, what recruiters observe, and what employers can change.
- **Leadership Search Brief:** market-mapping or assessment patterns without naming confidential clients.
- **Candidate Decision Brief:** anonymised patterns around notice periods, counteroffers, location, compensation or interview behaviour.

Where there is enough clean source data, quantify it. Where there is not, use qualitative language such as “we repeatedly see” rather than invented percentages.

## Publishing and reuse model

For each strong idea:
1. Capture the first-hand recruiter observation and supporting evidence.
2. Publish the canonical owned insight on HiredNext when appropriate.
3. Create a distinct media pitch or bylined version for one publication — never mass-copy the same article across sites.
4. Create a short founder LinkedIn version with a clear point of view.
5. Add genuinely earned external coverage to the media authority register.
6. Measure whether search impressions, referring domains, branded queries and AI citations improve over time.

## Measurement dashboard

Track monthly:
- New earned-media mentions and unique referring publications.
- Branded search impressions/clicks for HiredNext and Taru Shikha.
- Non-branded Search Console impressions for executive search, leadership hiring and sector recruitment topics.
- Indexed blog/authority URLs and crawl health.
- Media links that mention HiredNext/Taru Shikha explicitly versus generic unattributed mentions.
- AI-answer citations/mentions observed in repeatable benchmark prompts across major answer engines.
- Leads assisted by content/media where attribution is available.

Do not treat any one metric as a guaranteed ranking signal. The objective is stronger, independently corroborated expertise and discoverability.

## Guardrails

- No fabricated data, client names or outcomes.
- No paid low-quality link farms or mass guest-post networks.
- No keyword-stuffed rewrites of approved pages.
- No design changes to Home, Blog or Services without explicit approval.
- Do not claim AI replaces recruiters; HiredNext’s differentiated position is human judgement supported by technology.
- Keep candidate and employer services separate.
