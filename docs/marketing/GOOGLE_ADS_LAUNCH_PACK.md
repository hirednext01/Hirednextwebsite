# Search-term governance

Review search terms at least twice weekly during launch.

Classify every meaningful query as:

1. employer commercial intent — keep / promote,
2. candidate intent — negative,
3. research intent that can become an employer — assess by conversions,
4. irrelevant industry/geo intent — negative,
5. competitor comparison intent — retain if commercially relevant and ad copy remains factual.

Do not use competitor trademarks in ad copy unless legal/policy review permits it. Comparison content on the HiredNext site should remain evidence-led and should not make unsupported superiority claims.

---

# Bidding sequence

Phase 1: controlled launch

- Exact + phrase intent clusters
- conversion tracking verified
- tight geography and schedule aligned with employer audience
- avoid spreading budget across too many ad groups before search-term quality is known

Phase 2: conversion-led expansion

- promote winning queries
- add tested phrase variants
- consider broad match only after primary conversion signals are reliable and conversion-based Smart Bidding is appropriate
- use negative-keyword learnings aggressively

Phase 3: P&L optimisation

Optimise toward:

- cost per qualified employer lead,
- cost per booked employer discussion,
- qualified-lead-to-mandate conversion,
- pipeline value / spend,
- eventual gross profit / acquisition cost.

Pause keywords that produce candidate traffic, low-quality enquiries or research clicks without employer pipeline.

---

# Geography

Start with India and the locations where HiredNext can genuinely service employer mandates. Use Presence targeting rather than broad “interest in” geography where available/appropriate so budget is not wasted on irrelevant overseas jobseekers searching Indian roles.

Separate international employer acquisition only after India search economics are understood.

# Landing-page rule

Never send every ad to the homepage.

Send the searcher to the page that answers the query:

- general executive search → executive-search service
- compare firms → executive-search buyer guide
- CFO → CFO guide
- BFSI → BFSI authority page
- IT → IT authority page
- GCC → GCC authority page
- retail → Retail authority page
- manufacturing → Manufacturing authority page
- engineering → Engineering authority page
- RPO → RPO guide / service

This improves message match, measurement and conversion quality while the same pages compound organically.
