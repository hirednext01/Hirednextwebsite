# HiredNext Search Authority & Growth System

Updated: 2026-08-11

## 1. P&L objective

The objective is not traffic for its own sake. The marketing system should increase:

1. qualified employer enquiries,
2. booked hiring-mandate discussions,
3. executive-search / specialist-search / RPO opportunities,
4. attributable pipeline value,
5. organic and AI-assisted discovery that lowers acquisition cost over time.

Primary operating metrics:

- Qualified employer leads
- Booked employer calls
- Qualified-lead to mandate conversion
- Cost per qualified employer lead
- Cost per booked employer call
- Pipeline value / paid-search spend
- Organic non-brand enquiries
- AI referral sessions and resulting conversions

Do not optimise campaigns around raw clicks, impressions or follower count unless they support these outcomes.

## 3. Google Ads launch structure

Use Search first. Do not begin with broad display or awareness spend.

Google currently recommends exact match for the greatest steering/control and advises advertisers to expand with phrase/broad where performance supports it; broad match is best paired with conversion-based Smart Bidding. Start controlled, collect conversion data, then widen deliberately.

### Client-acquisition negative keyword baseline

Apply carefully and review the Search Terms report. Candidate-intent exclusions will usually include variants of:

- jobs
- vacancies
- vacancy
- fresher
- internship
- resume
- CV
- salary
- interview questions
- course
- training
- free
- apply
- naukri
- indeed

Do **not** exclude broad commercial terms such as `recruitment agency`, `executive recruitment`, `headhunter`, or `recruitment firm`; employers use them too.

Google notes that negative keywords behave differently from positive match types and do not automatically cover all singular/plural or synonym variants, so search-term review remains necessary.

## 4. Conversion setup before scaling paid traffic

Primary conversion actions:

1. completed employer contact form,
2. completed Calendly hiring-mandate booking.

Secondary diagnostic actions:

- employer email click,
- employer phone click,
- high-intent guide -> contact click,
- high-intent guide -> Calendly click.

Do not optimise bidding toward low-intent page views.

The site now emits an `ai_referral` GA event when a recognised AI referral source is detected and Google Analytics is configured. ChatGPT referrals using `utm_source=chatgpt.com` are normalised to `chatgpt`.

## 5. UTM naming convention

Use one convention everywhere:

`utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<format>&utm_campaign=<pillar>&utm_content=<asset>`

Examples:

- LinkedIn founder post: `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=executive_search&utm_content=compare_firms_founder`
- YouTube: `utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=cfo_search&utm_content=confidential_cfo_guide`
- Quora: `utm_source=quora&utm_medium=expert_answer&utm_campaign=rpo&utm_content=when_to_use_rpo`
- Reddit: `utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=expert_answer&utm_campaign=leadership_search&utm_content=passive_leaders`
- X: `utm_source=x&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=executive_search&utm_content=search_timeline`

## 6. LinkedIn operating model

### Founder account

Cadence: 3 substantive posts per week.

Content mix:

- 40% executive-search judgement and real hiring dilemmas
- 25% sector-specific hiring insight
- 20% employer decision education
- 15% founder perspective / leadership lessons

Every post should make one useful argument. Avoid generic motivational HR content.

### HiredNext company page

Cadence: 2 posts per week.

Content mix:

- decision-guide excerpts,
- role-market intelligence,
- anonymised evidence where permitted,
- new authority pages,
- open senior mandates where appropriate.

Do not duplicate the founder post word-for-word.

## 8. Reddit and Quora

Use them for expert participation, not disguised advertising.

Rules:

- Never fabricate a satisfied client or candidate identity.
- Never create fake independent recommendations for HiredNext.
- A founder, recruiter, company representative or clearly labelled professional account may answer relevant questions.
- Lead with a complete useful answer; link only when the HiredNext page genuinely adds depth.
- Do not paste the same answer across multiple threads.

Cadence: 2 high-quality answers per week across the two platforms is enough to begin.

Best question families:

- How should a company select an executive search firm in India?
- Is retained executive search worth it for a CFO?
- How do you recruit passive senior leaders?
- What is the difference between RPO and recruitment agencies?
- What should a board assess in a CFO candidate?
- How long should executive hiring take?

## 11. Content production flywheel

One authority topic should produce:

1. one definitive HiredNext website guide,
2. one founder LinkedIn post,
3. one company LinkedIn post,
4. one YouTube video,
5. two or three Shorts/Reels,
6. one X thread or several short posts,
7. one or two relevant Reddit/Quora expert answers,
8. internal links from relevant sector/service pages.

This prevents seven channels from becoming seven separate content factories.

## 12. Claim integrity

`app/Config/BrandFacts.php` is the source of record for public company claims.

Do not publish or strengthen company-wide totals, percentages, averages or speed claims unless they have a current verifiable source of record.

The public response layer removes legacy homepage references to unverified claims such as 1500+ placements, 98% success rate, 21-day speed, 12 sectors and 25+ industries until those figures are verified.

## 13. Weekly CEO review

Every week review:

- employer enquiries by source,
- booked mandate discussions,
- paid search terms and spend,
- qualified-lead rate by campaign,
- organic queries gaining impressions,
- guide pages gaining clicks,
- AI referral sessions,
- LinkedIn posts generating employer conversations,
- YouTube topics generating qualified site visits,
- pipeline value attributable to marketing.

Kill or change channels that consume work but do not create qualified conversations. Double down on query clusters that generate employer demand.
