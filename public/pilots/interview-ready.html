Operations Manager

  • Leads an 18-person warehouse team.
  • Introduced a written shift handover log.
  • Says fewer deliveries were missed, but has no records of the exact numbers.

This is an example, not a real candidate. Your pack would use your own experience.

“I was responsible for warehouse operations and managing the team.”
1. Say what you did

“I lead an 18-person warehouse team. I introduced a written handover log so the next shift could see what needed attention.” Use this structure with an example from your own work.

2. Explain the result honestly

“We saw fewer missed deliveries afterwards, but I do not have the exact numbers.” Describe what changed. Only use figures you can support.

3. Be ready for the next question

What did you put in the handover log? How did you get the team to use it? How did you check whether it helped? Think through these details before your interview.

4. Put your answer together

Explain the problem, what you did and what changed. Then practise saying it aloud. Could someone understand your part without reading your CV?
