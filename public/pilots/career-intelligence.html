Questions, answered clearly

Before you choose a service.

Which services can I buy today?

The ₹599 CV Assessment, ₹999 ATS CV Optimisation and ₹1,799 Professional CV Rebuild use HiredNext's existing secure request and payment flows. Other services on this private preview are collecting launch interest. HiredNext will confirm their final scope, price and availability before taking payment.

How does the complimentary first-100 reward work?

Eligibility would be verified from the partner voucher or campaign link. After confirmation, the customer would receive a secure HiredNext route to submit the CV directly. The complimentary allocation is limited to the first 100 verified redemptions and subject to the published campaign terms.

Will Zepto receive my CV or personal career details?

Your CV does not need to be sent through Zepto. The intended flow sends it directly to HiredNext after your consent. Zepto can receive aggregate or campaign-level reporting needed to measure the campaign, such as impressions, clicks and attributed conversions.

Are salary benchmarks exact?

No benchmark can promise one exact salary for every company. HiredNext would use the available evidence for the role, experience level, industry and city, state the limits of that evidence, and give you a range and decision context.

Do AI mentors replace human review?

No. AI can support structured practice, feedback and repeatable exercises. HiredNext defines the framework and guardrails, and services described as human-reviewed include human judgment in the stated scope.

Does buying a career service improve my chance of a job through HiredNext?

Career services are separate from recruitment consideration. Buying a service does not provide priority, guarantee shortlisting or promise an interview, offer or placement. Candidates can apply for open jobs without buying a service.
